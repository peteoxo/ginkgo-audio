Privacy Policy and Support. Last updated 16 September 2026.

Privacy

Ginkgo Audio is a local music player. We do not create an account for you, we do not run analytics, and we do not sell or share personal data.

  • Music files stay on your iPhone (or folders you choose). Playback does not upload your library.
  • Optional features you turn on (for example lyrics or cover-art lookup) send only the search needed to fetch that item from a public service. We do not keep a copy of those requests on our servers — we do not operate a user database.
  • Crash or hang logs stay on your device unless you send feedback yourself.

If you contact us by email, we use that message only to reply to your request.
